---
name: Private feedback access
description: Why support access is free but account-scoped rather than a public comment feed.
---

Feedback is available to signed-in accounts without requiring a paid membership. Ticket discussions are private to the submitting account and the configured app owners.

**Why:** Users need a way to report issues or request changes even when they have not purchased membership. Requiring sign-in provides a private reply/history channel and basic abuse control without publishing users' reports to everyone.

**How to apply:** Keep support independent of projection and Game Takes paywalls. Only owners may review all tickets, write responses or change statuses. Do not turn support into a public community feed or promise email alerts without separately implementing notification delivery.
