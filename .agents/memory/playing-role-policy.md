Current-role evidence must govern expected slate workload and DFS value recommendations across sports.

**Why:** The user reported a backup quarterback ranked above the established starter because the model treated the backup's past starts as expected production for the upcoming game. They specifically requested team-depth research across all supported sports.

**How to apply:** Use published depth order for football quarterbacks, game-specific assignments for starting goalies and pitchers, batting orders when published, and recorded recent workload for rotations. A basketball bench player can legitimately be more useful than a nominal starter; do not blindly force starters above productive rotations. Race entries replace team-depth concepts for NASCAR.

Label derived or unknown roles honestly where published depth information is unavailable. Unsupported participation must not produce an eligible full-game value pick or invented zero-point forecast. Do not invent arbitrary backup workload percentages, use provider fantasy projections, or apply current depth charts to historical slates. Current role checks and historical stat inputs have different timestamps; preserve their provenance.
