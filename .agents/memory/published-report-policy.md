Authorized report access must be confirmed before importing publisher data. Owner attestation is not independent publisher authentication or a grant of redistribution rights. Do not bypass publisher gates to fill coverage.

**Why:** The user requires approved report access, and public FantasyPros coverage may contain only a ten-team preview. Missing teams are intentional unavailable states.

**How to apply:** Keep PFF secondary strength and FantasyPros positional fantasy allowance distinct, including opposite rank-one directions. Never infer unavailable teams or silently change ALLPLAY's independent forecast means from these reports. Treat covered weeks as explicitly declared applicability, not permission to extrapolate a weekly rank from a season aggregate.
