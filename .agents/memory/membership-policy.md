Charge $1.99 USD monthly for player projections only for regular members. News and daily slates remain free. The designated owner is exempt from the membership fee.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this price, billing cycle and paid scope when connecting Whop, then approved private owner access without a monthly payment.

**How to apply:** Do not turn the entire dashboard into a paid-only app or change the fee without approval. Whop is the purchase/access authority for regular members; the owner exception must remain operator-designated and server-verified. Keep projections out of public API responses and shared visitor caches.
